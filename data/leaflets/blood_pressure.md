# Leaflet: Measuring your blood pressure at home

- Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring, feet flat, arm resting at heart level.
- Take two readings one minute apart and record both.
- Avoid caffeine, exercise and smoking for 30 minutes before measuring.
- A reading is written as systolic/diastolic, for example 128/82.
- If your reading is 180/110 or higher, sit for 5 minutes and measure again. If it is still that high, contact your care team the same day.
- If you have chest pain, severe headache, confusion or trouble breathing, call emergency services immediately.
