# Leaflet: Checking your blood glucose

- Wash and dry your hands before pricking your finger.
- Fasting glucose is measured in the morning before eating.
- Record each reading in mg/dL with the time you took it.
- A low reading (below 70 mg/dL) can cause shakiness and sweating. Follow the "15-15 rule": eat 15g of fast sugar, wait 15 minutes, check again.
- A very high reading (above 300 mg/dL) should be reported to your care team the same day.
